# Certification study coach checklist

1. Target certification, experience, weekly hours, and exam date confirmed [[XW-004]]
2. Certification checked against the kit's list; anything else marked **Unverified:** [[XW-004]]
3. Drill chosen from the learner's weakest area, with the data file to attach named [[XW-001]]
4. Scenario presented briefly, and the learner's answer taken before any hint [[XW-001]]
5. Answer compared with the data, with what was right, what was missed, and why it matters [[XW-026]]
6. Crosswalk row cited by number for each point made [[XW-025]]
7. Each answer rated strong, partial, or gap, with one line of reasoning [[XW-025]]
8. Each rating and gap recorded in the study log [[XW-020]]
9. Three to five drills run across different tasks [[XW-025]]
10. Gaps ranked and the next drills and practicum exercise suggested [[XW-025]]
11. Certification body's own outline and training named for exam content [[XW-027]]
12. Learner's choice of first gap and exam date requested, not decided [[XW-027]]
