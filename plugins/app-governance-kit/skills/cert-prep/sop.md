# Certification study coach SOP

## Purpose

Give a learner repeated, low-stakes practice on the on-the-job tasks behind an ITAM or AI-governance certification, with an honest view of the gaps. It supports study. It does not replace the certification body's course, and it is not exam content.

## Scope

Applies to the six certifications in the kit's field guide: CAMP, CSAM, CHAMP, AIGP, ITIL 4 Foundation, and the IAM Certificate. Drills use the fictional Halden Logistics demo estate only. Do not paste real company data into a study session.

## Roles

- **Learner** — answers each drill before seeing the data comparison, and chooses what to study next.
- **Coach** — the agent or a person running the drills, rating answers, and keeping the log.
- **Manager or sponsor** — agrees study time and funds the exam, if the employer pays.

## Frequency

One session of 30 to 45 minutes a week until the exam, plus a final readiness session about a week before the exam date.

## Steps

1. Confirm the target certification, the learner's experience, weekly hours, and exam date [[XW-004]]
2. Check the certification against the kit's list; mark anything else **Unverified:** [[XW-004]]
3. Pick a drill from the learner's weakest area and name the data file to attach [[XW-001]]
4. Present the scenario and take the learner's answer before giving any hint [[XW-001]]
5. Compare the answer with the data and explain what was right, what was missed, and why it matters [[XW-026]]
6. Cite the crosswalk row by number for each point [[XW-025]]
7. Rate each answer strong, partial, or gap, with one line of reasoning [[XW-025]]
8. Record each rating and gap in the study log [[XW-020]]
9. Run three to five drills across different tasks in the session [[XW-025]]
10. Rank the gaps and suggest the next drills and the matching practicum exercise [[XW-025]]
11. Name the certification body's own outline and training as the source for exam content [[XW-027]]
12. Ask the learner to choose the first gap to work and whether to book the exam [[XW-027]]

## Evidence to retain

- The study log: date, certification, task, rating, gap, and next drill for every drill.
- The ranked gap list from each session.
- The learner's chosen next step and exam date.
