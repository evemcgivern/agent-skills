# Fit check (optional, before drafting)

Purpose: before the candidate invests in a tailored resume, show where their supplied resume does and does not support the posting. It is an evidence read of two documents. It is not a verdict on the candidate and not a prediction.

## When to run it

- The candidate asks whether to apply, whether they fit, or whether a role is right for them: deliver only the fit check. Do not start a resume build.
- During a resume build, once the source ledger exists: if the posting states a requirement as required and the ledger grades it absent or unclear, give the fit check once before drafting. It informs the candidate and does not block supported work.
- Otherwise skip it. Do not run it unasked when the stated requirements are supported.

Inputs are the original resume, the complete job description, and the candidate's confirmed answers. Without the job description there is nothing to check against; without the resume nothing can be graded. Mark the affected parts UNVERIFIED.

## Method

1. **Role reality.** In three to five lines, say what the person would do day to day and with whom, using the posting text only. Label each line **stated** (taken from the posting) or **inferred** (your reading, with the basis). Flag a mismatch when the title and the described duties point to different jobs. Do not research the employer here. A company fact appears only if the candidate supplied it or an available trustworthy source confirms it, and the source is named.
2. **Requirement table.** One row per requirement in the posting.

   | Requirement (posting wording) | Posting says | Evidence | Resume reference or candidate answer |
   |---|---|---|---|

   - *Posting says* is **required**, **preferred**, or **unstated**, taken only from the posting's wording ("required", "must", "minimum", "N+ years"). Never decide a requirement is hard by guessing.
   - *Evidence* uses the ledger grades: direct, adjacent, absent, unclear.
   - Put required rows with absent or unclear evidence first.
   - Check quantified requirements (years, certifications, licences, clearance, work authorization, location) against the dates and credentials exactly as supplied, and show the arithmetic. Adjacent experience does not count toward a quantified requirement; say so.
3. **Likely screening gaps.** List at most three places where a reader comparing the resume with the posting would see a gap, each tied to a table row. State them as observations from the evidence. Give no probability, percentage, ranking, or statement that the candidate will be rejected.
4. **Options.** Offer next steps and let the candidate choose: proceed with tailoring (unsupported items stay out of the resume and go in the notes); share evidence for the unclear rows; or reconsider the role. Do not suggest wording that would imply experience the candidate does not have. A candidate answer that closes a gap is recorded in the ledger with provenance, as usual.

## Rules

- No overall score, percent, or "match" label. Per-requirement grades only.
- No hiring prediction and no ATS promise.
- Company legitimacy and pay ranges are out of scope. If asked, say so. Research them only with an available trustworthy source, cite it, and label anything unsourced UNVERIFIED. Give a pay figure only when the candidate supplied it or a cited source supports it.
- One fit check per role. Never reuse another role's table.
- Keep the fit check in the notes, separate from any resume or letter. Mark it Draft if an input was incomplete.

## Worked example (synthetic)

Resume: six years as an operations analyst, with reporting, process documentation, and no named ERP product. Posting: "Business Systems Analyst". Duties: draft proposals and statements of work, run client workshops, gather requirements. Requires 3+ years with AcmeERP. Prefers PMP.

**Role reality**
- Writes proposals and statements of work. *Stated.*
- Runs client workshops and gathers requirements. *Stated.*
- The title says analyst, but two of the three duties are client-facing writing and workshops. *Inferred from the duty list; the posting does not give time splits.*

| Requirement | Posting says | Evidence | Resume reference or answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3+ years AcmeERP | required | absent | No ERP named in any role. Ask whether it was used and for how long. |
| Draft proposals and statements of work | unstated (duty) | unclear | Resume mentions "process documentation" only. |
| Gather requirements | unstated (duty) | adjacent | Role 2, bullet 3: "interviewed stakeholders to document reporting needs". |
| PMP | preferred | absent | No credential listed. |

**Likely screening gaps:** the AcmeERP years requirement has no support (row 1); proposal writing is not evidenced (row 2).

**Options:** proceed with tailoring around requirements gathering; tell me if you used AcmeERP or wrote proposals; or reconsider the role. Your call.
