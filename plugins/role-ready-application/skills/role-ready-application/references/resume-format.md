# Resume format

This is a presentation default, not a source of candidate facts. The candidate may request another style. Populate only from the current candidate's source ledger. Preserve distinctive, supported achievements and context; remove duplication before cutting unique relevant evidence. Ask about unclear facts. Never infer content from a sample resume.

## Section order and content

1. Center the candidate's name, bold, 17 pt, navy. Use confirmed spelling/capitalization.
2. Center one compact contact line with only supplied location, phone, email, professional profile. Separate with ` | `; omit missing fields and dangling separators. Keep link text visible in the document body. The name and contact line are the first lines of page 1, as plain text with no icons or photo. If the source has no phone or no email, ask once before delivering. Ask once whether a portfolio, GitHub or project site supports this role, and add only a link the candidate supplies. Omit marital status, nationality, age, street address and any detail not relevant to the role; if the source carries one, flag it in the notes and ask. Omit a photo unless the candidate asks, since expectations vary by country and industry.
3. Center an uppercase, bold, navy specialty line using supported strengths relevant to the role, separated by ` · `. It is not a new employment title and must not echo every JD keyword. Add a thin navy bottom border.
4. Add one concise opening profile paragraph, no “Summary” heading by default. Use direct, concrete supported scope, work, and outcomes. Follow SKILL.md's theme matching and profile rules for what the profile leads with. First-person narrative is the default unless candidate requests another voice. Do not invent total years or achievements; SKILL.md step 3 governs what the profile, specialty line and skills groups may contain.
5. `CORE SKILLS`: grouped paragraphs with bold category labels followed by supported capabilities separated by ` | `. Choose categories for this candidate; avoid repetitive keyword lists.
6. `PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE`: reverse chronological. Role line: bold official title, employer/location, bold dates; show month and year for the start and end of each role (March 2022 – June 2024) and `Present` for a current role. If the source gives only a year, ask once and keep what the candidate supplies. Keep the dates in the same paragraph, after the right tab. If a long title/employer wraps, the dates sit at the right margin of its last line; if they do not fit beside that line they drop to a line of their own. Do not split the dates into a separate paragraph. Add brief gray italic context only when supported. Use round achievement/responsibility bullets. Keep role line, context, and first bullet together. Older positions may be summarized under a bold `Earlier:` label when material facts and chronology remain clear.
7. Optional `SHIPPED PRODUCTS & OPEN SOURCE` only for supported, relevant shipped/open-source work. Use project names and supported descriptions. Use `PROJECTS` for other projects. Open source and AI-assisted projects are never required: for a candidate or target that is non-technical or not a developer, leave them off unless the candidate asks for them. Omit unsupported sections. When this work is the best evidence for one of the posting's top three themes, place it after the skills and before `PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE` (SKILL.md, Theme matching); otherwise keep it after the experience.
8. Optional `TECHNICAL SKILLS` (for a senior or technical target, ask once whether to merge it with `CORE SKILLS` into one `SKILLS` block of 4 to 6 labeled groups after the profile; public advice on skills placement is mixed, so keep the default split unless the candidate agrees): grouped tool/skill lines with bold labels and ` | ` separators. Avoid duplicating all skills terms; omit if unsupported.
9. Select education/credential heading from actual entries:
   - Education/programs/coursework and no certifications: `EDUCATION`.
   - Certifications and no education: `CERTIFICATIONS`.
   - Both: `EDUCATION & CERTIFICATION`.
   - Neither: omit the entire section and all sublabels.
   A course is not automatically a certification. Preserve exact names and classification; ask if materially ambiguous. In-progress certifications may determine the heading but must remain marked unearned. A `Certifications:` sublabel appears only for earned certificates; an `In progress:` line only for explicitly supplied ongoing ones. Never infer a credential from the JD.

Keep questions, job-fit notes, evidence references, change notes, and missing qualifications outside the resume, and never put a DRAFT banner or check status inside the resume text; the Draft label belongs in the filename and the notes. Never reorder roles: keep reverse chronological order by end date; if the source itself is out of date order, keep its order and ask. Never add an empty heading or placeholder.

## DOCX defaults

| Element | Default |
|---|---|
| Page | US Letter, portrait |
| Margins | 0.5 in top/bottom; 0.625 in left/right; never below 0.5 in |
| Typeface | Calibri; metrically similar sans serif only if unavailable |
| Body/supporting text | 11 pt, black, left aligned; single spacing |
| Name | 17 pt, bold, navy `#1F3864`, centered; 1 pt after |
| Contact | 11 pt, centered; 1 pt after; visible link text |
| Specialty | 11 pt, bold, navy, centered; 4 pt after; thin navy bottom paragraph border |
| Profile | 11 pt; 1.0 line spacing; 3 pt before/after |
| Section headings | 11 pt, bold, uppercase, navy; 6.5 pt before, 2.6 pt after; 0.75 pt gray `#808080` bottom paragraph border, 2 pt border spacing |
| Grouped skills/technical/education lines | 11 pt; bold category label, regular values; 1.0 line spacing; 0 pt before, 2.2 pt after |
| Role line | 11 pt; bold title and dates; regular employer/location; 5.2 pt before, 0.4 pt after; right tab at the text width (7.25 in on US Letter with 0.625 in side margins; recompute if margins change), no leader if available |
| Context | 11 pt, italic, gray `#404040`; 1.0 line spacing; 0 pt before, 2.5 pt after |
| Bullets | 11 pt, black; 1.0 line spacing; 0 pt before, 1.4 pt after; left indent 0.15 in, hanging indent 0.125 in; real Word list numbering |
| Earlier-career paragraph | 11 pt; bold introductory label; 1.0 line spacing; 5 pt before, 2 pt after |

Use editable paragraphs/runs, paragraph borders and defined styles. Do not make layout out of images, tables, floating shapes, text boxes, or columns. Do not put important text or contacts in headers/footers. Keep headings with next paragraph; avoid separated role headings and unnecessary bullet splits without keeping entire long experience sections together and creating blank space. Do not add repeating contact headers or page furniture by default.

If a font substitution or candidate content creates clipping, fix wrapping/pagination first and increase type size or line spacing as needed for legibility. Keep bullets together where practical without creating large blank areas. Never leave a single line alone at the top or bottom of a page. When a whole bullet would sit alone at a page edge, set keep-with-next on the second-to-last bullet of the role so the last two bullets move together. Use these compact reference measurements as starting points, then judge the rendered document.

## Length and accessible fallback

Maximum two **rendered resume pages**; one is fine. Aim for one page when the candidate has under about five years of relevant experience, and use a second page only when supported content fills at least half of it. Two pages are usual for senior and technical roles. Keep the opening profile within about 5 rendered lines and each bullet within about 3 lines; split or tighten a longer one before cutting lines by score. Font steps, margins, overflow handling and the line-cutting score are in SKILL.md's length and presentation gate. No text, including contacts, goes below 10 pt unless the candidate chose a smaller size. Never call an overlength document compliant.

Plain text uses the same information order, uppercase section labels, grouped labels, role/employer/location/date order, context and bullets; visual styling can be omitted. Deliver a complete copyable resume without requiring paid tools. Plain text cannot verify pagination. Disclose formatting limits outside the resume.

## Final check

Apply the checklist in SKILL.md's length and presentation gate to each delivered DOCX/PDF. Extract each rendition including link labels and compare order, contacts, names, titles, dates, credentials, and bullets to the source ledger. Recompute credential headings from final entries. Verify actual Word numbering indentation as well as style indentation. If rendering or extraction is unavailable, report exactly what is UNVERIFIED and label the artifact Draft. Never claim ATS certification.
